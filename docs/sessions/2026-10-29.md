## Follow-up — detail image gallery

### Changed
- Clicking a displayed card image opens a centered, horizontal swipe gallery over the timeline.
- The gallery fits images inside a 75%-height viewport, reports the current image for multi-image
  items, and uses the existing back control to dismiss it.
- The detail viewer shares the timeline texture cache, requests only the current and adjacent images,
  and releases its requests and leases when closed. Timeline slideshow cards pause while it is open,
  retaining their displayed images underneath the overlay.
- Fixed timeline chrome is beneath the overlay; the back control is raised above it to close the gallery,
  and footer category controls are temporarily disabled.
- `overview.md` documents the detail viewer and its layering.

### Decisions
- Open the gallery on the image currently shown by the card → the visitor's click and the first detail
  image correspond even while the card slideshow is cycling.
- Use a horizontal `ScrollRect` with one centered page per image → supports the project's touchscreen
  swipe interaction and mouse dragging while preserving complete images.
- Load only the current page and its immediate neighbors → long galleries do not pin every decoded
  texture in memory.
